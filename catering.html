Catering

We will cook it and bring it

Office lunches, graduations, showers, game days. The same kitchen that cooks the dining room, packed hot and ready to serve.

Call (215) 654-5002 See the menus

Three ways to do it

  • Trays, dropped off

    Half and full trays of the plates you already order — enchiladas, fajitas, carnitas, rice and beans — hot in the pan, ready to set out. The simplest way to feed a room.

  • The taco bar

    Fillings, tortillas, salsas and all the garnishes laid out so people build their own. Works for twenty people and it works for eighty.

  • Full service

    We bring the food, set it up, keep it stocked and clear it away. For the events where you would rather not be the one refilling the chafing dish.

How to book it

Call the restaurant and ask for catering. We will talk through head count, what you want on the table, whether you need it dropped off or set up, and what time it has to be ready.

A few days' notice is usually enough for trays. Full service and anything over fifty people is better with a week or two. If it is shorter notice than that, call anyway — sometimes we can.

Vegetarian and gluten-free are never a problem, but tell us up front so the kitchen builds them in rather than working around them.

Before you call

  • Head count, even a rough one
  • Date and the time it must be ready
  • Drop-off or full service
  • Any allergies or dietary needs
  • Whether you need serving equipment

Add pricing, minimums and lead times
