What is on every week

Open for lunch Tuesday through Sunday, 11 AM to 3 PM. Here is the rest of what is on — call if you want to be sure before you drive out.

Every week

  • Every TuesdayLunch & dinner

    All-you-can-eat tacos — $18

    One day a week, the taco basket keeps coming. Eighteen dollars, as many as you can manage.

  • Tue–Sat4:30 – 6:30 PMSun 3:30 – 5:30 PM

    Happy hour

    Bar and patio only. Seven-dollar margaritas, three-dollar tacos, half-off wines, four- and five-dollar drafts, and the short list of aperitivos.

    One day a week the kitchen turns to eggs — huevos rancheros, dulce de leche French toast, pancakes and a breakfast burrito.
