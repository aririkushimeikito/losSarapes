Menus & hours

Welcome to Los Sarapes

For twenty-five years we have brought delectable, authentic, homemade cuisine from Mexico City and the surrounding states to Bucks and Montgomery County.

  • The patio

    Covered, shaded, and open all summer — the best seat we have.

  • The bar

    Margaritas mixed to order and a tequila list worth reading twice.

  • Takeout

    Call ahead or order online and we'll have it hot at the host stand.

The kitchen

Seven menus, one kitchen

The bar

A tequila list worth reading twice

Every margarita is shaken to order with fresh lime — no mix, no shortcuts. Behind them sits a shelf we have spent years filling.

See the cocktail menu
  • Blanco Unaged and green-peppery. What the house margarita is built on.
  • Reposado A few months in oak. Softer, a little vanilla, easy to sip.
  • Añejo A year or more down. Order it neat and take your time.
  • Mezcal Smoke from the roasting pit. Ask and we will pour you a taste.

Outside

The best seat we have

Covered, shaded and quiet enough to hear the person across from you. When the weather turns, it is the first table to go.

  • Covered and shaded, so the sun is never the problem
  • Full menu and the full bar, served outside
  • Big tables for big groups — call ahead and we will set them up
  • First come, first served, weather permitting
