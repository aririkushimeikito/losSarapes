The bar

A tequila list worth reading twice

Every margarita is shaken to order with fresh lime — no mix, no shortcuts. Behind them sits a shelf we have spent years filling.

Pull up a stool
  1. 01 Blanco Unaged and green-peppery. What the house margarita is built on.
  2. 02 Reposado A few months in oak. Softer, a little vanilla, easy to sip.
  3. 03 Añejo A year or more down. Order it neat and take your time.
  4. 04 Mezcal Smoke from the roasting pit. Ask and we will pour you a taste.
  5. 05 The house margarita Blanco, lime, orange liqueur, salt if you want it. Nothing else.
