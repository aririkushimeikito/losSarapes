Menus

Seven menus, one kitchen

Masa pressed in house, salsas roasted each morning, moles built from whole chiles. Which one you want depends mostly on what time it is.

  • Lunch

    Tuesday to Friday, 11:30 AM – 3 PM

    Smaller plates, out of the kitchen fast, so an hour is enough.

  • Dinner

    From 3 PM until close

    The full kitchen — the parrilla, the moles, the plates that take all morning.

  • Happy hour

    Tuesday to Friday, 4 – 6:30 PM

    Bar and patio only. Same kitchen, smaller plates, better prices.

  • Cocktails

    Whenever the bar is open

    Margaritas shaken to order, and the tequila shelf behind them.

  • Beers & drafts

    Draft list rotates

    Mexican lagers, local drafts, horchata and aguas frescas.

  • Sunday brunch

    Sundays, 12 – 3 PM

    Chilaquiles, huevos rancheros, and a very large michelada.

  • Kids

    All day

    Under twelve. Smaller plates, nothing spicy, and it comes out first.

Vegetarian, gluten-free and kid-sized versions of most plates — just ask your server and the kitchen will sort it out. Marked V for vegetarian and GF for gluten-free.
