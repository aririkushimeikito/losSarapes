About

Welcome to Los Sarapes Horsham

Our name is metaphoric. A sarape is a blanket that warms you — we want the food you eat here to taste delicious, and the welcome to be just as warm.

1101 Horsham Rd, Ambler, PA 19002

How it started

The Los Sarapes family of restaurants was born from the entrepreneurial spirit of Carlos and Olga Marin in 1992, after they bought a pizza shop in Chalfont and ran it with their children and spouses. When they introduced some of their family's favourite dishes from their native Mexico, customers were delighted.

Everything since has come from the same kitchen and the same family. Three more restaurants, three more generations of cooks, and the same insistence on traditional dishes made with the freshest ingredients.

Where we are now

At the present location, owners Luis, Mauricio and Alfonso bring that same traditional food to a much larger room — two covered patios outside and a full bar in.

We welcome you to taste our piece of Mexico in Horsham.

The family

Four restaurants, one kitchen

  1. 1992

    A pizza shop in Chalfont

    Carlos and Olga Marin buy it and run it with their children and spouses, then start cooking the dishes they grew up with. That turns out to be the business.

  2. 2000

    El Sarape, Blue Bell

    Son Luis and his wife Marion open it, keeping to the traditional dishes prepared with the freshest ingredients.

    elsarapebluebell.com

  3. 2002

    Cilantro, Salem, Massachusetts

    Son Juan Carlos' wife Esther opens it with their family, taking the cooking further from home than it had been before.

  4. 2005

    Los Sarapes Horsham

    Son Mauricio and his wife Amy, with daughter Karina's husband Alfonso, open the original small location — the one that grew into the room you are sitting in now.

Los Sarapes Chalfont is still going too, run by the same family.
