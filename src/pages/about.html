About

Cook. Taste. Inspire.

For twenty-five years we have brought delectable, authentic, homemade cuisine from Mexico City and the surrounding states of Mexico to Bucks and Montgomery County.

Where the food comes from

Mexico City cooking is not one thing. It is what the whole country brings to the capital and leaves behind — mole from Puebla, carnitas from Michoacán, seafood off both coasts, and the street food that grew up in between. That is the range we cook from.

Masa is pressed in house. Salsas are roasted each morning rather than opened. Mole takes two days and we make it anyway, because the shortcut version is not the same dish. Nothing on the line comes out of a bag.

Twenty-five years in one neighborhood

A quarter century is long enough that we are cooking for the children of the first people who sat down here. Regulars have a table. Some have an order we start before they reach it.

We are five minutes off the Turnpike with free parking at the door, which matters more than it should. Walk-ins are always welcome, and the patio is covered and shaded and open all summer.
