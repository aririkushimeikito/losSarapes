Events

What is on this week

Some of it runs every week. Some of it depends on the season and the weather. Call if you want to be sure before you drive out.

Every week

  • Tuesday–Friday4:30 – 6:30 PM

    Happy hour

    Bar and patio only. House margaritas, drafts, well cocktails and wine, plus the short list of bar plates — street tacos, flautas, chips with queso.

    See the happy hour menu

  • Sunday11 AM – 3 PM

    Brunch

    One day a week the kitchen turns to eggs. Chilaquiles, huevos rancheros, machaca, a churro waffle, and a michelada the size of the afternoon.

    See the brunch menu

  • All summerWeather permitting

    The patio

    Covered and shaded, full menu and full bar served outside. First come, first served — it is the first section to fill.

    More about the patio

Holidays and one-offs

Cinco de Mayo, Día de los Muertos, Mother's Day, New Year's Eve — the ones worth cooking something special for, we do. Hours change on holidays, sometimes at short notice.
