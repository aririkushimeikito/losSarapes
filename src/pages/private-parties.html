Private parties

Planning an event?

Let us assist in arranging your next special event at Los Sarapes Horsham, or off premises. It will be our pleasure to coordinate all of the essential elements for your event, to ensure an unforgettable experience.

Birthdays, rehearsal dinners, showers, retirements, work nights out — big tables for big groups. Call ahead and we will set them up.

How groups usually go

  • A long table

    We push tables together in the dining room and give you a server of your own. Order off the regular menu. Easiest option for a birthday or a dinner with friends.

  • A set menu

    Pick two or three plates per course ahead of time and the kitchen runs it as one service. Faster on the night, and everyone knows the bill before they sit down.

  • The patio

    Covered and shaded, with the full menu and full bar served outside. The nicest room we have when the weather cooperates.

Planning it

Call and ask for whoever is handling parties. Tell us the date, the head count and roughly what you want the night to be — a sit-down dinner, drinks and small plates, or a room full of people eating standing up. We will tell you what fits.

Bar tabs can be open, capped at a number, or closed with drinks on people's own checks. Say which up front and we will run it that way all night rather than sorting it out at the end.

Cake, decorations and a playlist are all fine. Tell us if you are bringing them so we leave you the space and the time to set up.

What we will ask

  • Date and time
  • Head count, and how firm it is
  • Seated dinner or standing
  • Set menu or order off the menu
  • How you want the bar handled
  • Allergies and dietary needs
